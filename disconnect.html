Disconnecting from QuickBooks Online

Merchant First Reconciliation

Access granted to Merchant First Reconciliation can be withdrawn at any time, by either of the steps below. Either one is sufficient; doing both is the complete cleanup.

1. Revoke access in QuickBooks Online

In QuickBooks Online, open Settings (the gear icon) → Apps, find Merchant First Reconciliation in the list of connected apps, and choose Disconnect. This invalidates the application's tokens for that company file immediately. The application can make no further calls against it until it is authorized again.

2. Remove the stored tokens

On the computer where the application is installed, delete the token file in the application's secrets folder. This removes the stored credentials from that machine.

What happens to data already retrieved

Disconnecting stops any further retrieval. Reconciliation workpapers already produced are accounting records of Merchant First and are retained under the same records-retention policy that covers the underlying bank statements and books. See the Privacy Policy.
