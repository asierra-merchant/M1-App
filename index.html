Merchant First Reconciliation

An internal accounting tool of Merchant First LLC.

What it does

Merchant First Reconciliation prepares monthly bank reconciliation workpapers for Merchant First LLC and its affiliated entities. It reads bank statements and bank transaction exports, retrieves the corresponding transaction detail from QuickBooks Online, and produces a reconciliation workbook for each bank account showing the book balance rolled forward against the balance reported by the bank, together with a list of differences.

Who uses it

The application is used solely by employees and engaged professionals of Merchant First LLC and its affiliated entities, working on those entities' own books. It is not distributed to the public, offered for sale, or made available to any other organization.

How it connects to QuickBooks Online

The application connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit's published API using OAuth 2.0, under the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope. It reads transaction and account data. It does not create, modify or delete anything in QuickBooks Online. QuickBooks Online remains the system of record; this application produces workpapers from it.
